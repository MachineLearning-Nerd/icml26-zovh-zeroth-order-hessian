# Publication gate

This repository is ready to publish as a **bounded evidence audit**, not as a claim that the six paper claims were reproduced.

- Paper-level claims independently verified: **0/6**
- Finite proxy diagnostics passed: **3/4**
- Negative finite diagnostics: **1**
- Not reproduced: **C5 MNIST attack, C6 synthetic optimization speedup**
- Overall status: **INCONCLUSIVE**

The gate is generated by:

~~~bash
python3 repro/src/verify.py
python3 repro/src/finalize_gate.py
~~~

[outputs/gate.json](outputs/gate.json) is the authoritative machine-readable report. A passing publication gate means that the documented raw diagnostics and their conservative interpretation agree. It does not mean that the paper’s general theorems, query-reuse method, optimizer, or experiments have been independently reproduced.
